Bank of Russia Warning Against MaziFinance

The most significant finding in our investigation comes from the Bank of Russia.

Its official warning-list entry identifies Mazifinance, gives mazifinance.com as the website and states: "Signs of illegal professional securities market participant." The entry is dated 12 September 2025.

Why this matters:

Traders should not confuse a company's registration in a particular jurisdiction with authorization to provide regulated investment services. A company can have a corporate registration while lacking the regulatory authorization required to offer financial services to clients in a particular country.

One common mistake traders make is assuming that registration of a company automatically means the company is authorized to provide investment services.

If MaziFinance Asks You for Additional Money

Traders should be particularly careful if they encounter demands for additional payments before a withdrawal can supposedly be processed.

Be cautious about requests described as taxes, verification charges, insurance payments, account activation fees, withdrawal fees, compliance deposits, security deposits or other payments required before accessing funds.

Never assume that paying another fee guarantees that your existing balance will be released.

Before making any additional payment, independently verify the request with the relevant financial regulator and the actual regulated entity involved.

If You Have Already Deposited Money

If you have already deposited funds with MaziFinance and are concerned about your account, avoid making additional deposits simply because someone tells you that another payment is necessary to withdraw your existing funds.

  1. Stop sending additional money until the situation is verified.
  2. Save screenshots of your account and transaction history.
  3. Keep emails, WhatsApp messages and other communications.
  4. Record the wallet addresses or bank details used for payments.
  5. Contact your bank or payment provider immediately if appropriate.
  6. Report suspected financial fraud to the relevant authorities.
  7. Be cautious of anyone promising guaranteed recovery of lost funds.

Beware of Recovery Scams

Traders who lose money to an online investment platform can become targets for a second scam. Fraudsters may claim that they can recover your funds in exchange for an upfront fee, tax payment or legal charge.

Recovery warning:

Never assume that someone claiming to be a "fund recovery agent", "blockchain investigator" or "financial recovery specialist" can recover your money simply because they know details about your previous investment.
